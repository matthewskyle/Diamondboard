# Coach and Player Needs, Pain Points, and Unmet Wants in Baseball/Softball Whiteboard Apps (2024–2026)

Research method note: about 17 search/fetch calls (Nov 2026). Reddit content did not come up. Every `site:reddit.com` query returned non-Reddit results, so I found no direct Reddit quotes. Forum evidence comes from DiscussFastpitch (2024). App Store review evidence is thin and often old, because most baseball whiteboard apps have very few ratings. Many sources are vendor pages, and I label them as such.

## What do coaches use whiteboards/apps for?

### Takeaway
Coaches use these tools mainly to answer "where do I go?" on balls in play: backups, cutoffs, relays, and coverages. They use them in pre-practice or pre-game walk-throughs, in between-inning or dugout meetings, and to reinforce learning at home. Keeping absent assistant coaches and parents informed is a secondary use. Lineups, rotations, and practice planning sit in adjacent apps, and their reviewers have specific complaints about fairness.

### Cited Findings
- On DiscussFastpitch (Oct 7, 2024), a coach asked for a single resource on positioning after the ball is in play. The example question was where the LF goes on a ball down the RF line with one out and a runner on second. Replies pointed to InMotion Playbooks, to BASIQs ("more quiz-based than graphics / diagrams"), and to a PDF of defensive situations "we used for years with younger players." — [DiscussFastpitch thread](https://discussfastpitch.com/threads/good-resource-for-learning-how-to-coach-moving-without-the-ball.46674/)
- Dugout Edge, a vendor in beta, says its tool is used in practice, pre-game meetings, and remote sessions with parents and assistant coaches. It lists these pain points: diagramming on a physical board is slow, players "freeze" when a play develops, players need to know cutoff/relay/coverage jobs before games, fundamentals need reinforcing at home, and assistant coaches miss practices. — [Dugout Edge Chalkboard](https://www.dugoutedge.com/chalkboard)
- Pitch MetRx (vendor) positions its whiteboard for diagramming cutoffs and relays on a tablet during defensive meetings or between innings. — [Pitch MetRx Coach's Whiteboard](https://www.pitchmetrx.com/coaches-whiteboard/) (from search summary)
- Simple Fielding (a free competitor) frames its core question as "Where do I go?" and calls backing up bases "the job kids forget." It supports walking the team through plays before practice. — [Simple Fielding](https://simplefielding.com/)
- InMotion Playbooks (vendor) offers 256 animated defensive rotations ("set the field, choose a scenario, press play"). It supports review on desktop, presentation on a large video monitor, or a tablet version on the field. It cites a testimonial from coach Tim Walton: "easy to use and very detailed... change the way we communicate and coach our players." — [InMotion Playbooks](https://inmotionplaybooks.com/)
- In the lineup/rotation app Baseball Softball Pocket Coach ($4.99, 4.7★, 515 ratings), reviewers raised several issues. The random position generator "sometimes it will bench the same kid 3 times and not at all on others" (9/19/2021). They wanted positions to switch automatically when a player is marked absent, and they wanted to print positions on a diamond. — [App Store: Pocket Coach](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id633644009?l=en-us)
- Solis Baseball Positions targets the moment when kids don't know where they're playing. A clipboard of positions per inning in the dugout lets a child "know where to go without having to ask the coach." — [App Store: Solis Baseball Positions](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1476105094) (from search summary)

### Inferences
- Diamondboard's main job is the "after the ball is hit, who goes where" walk-through. Its per-step notes and step animation fit this directly.
- Dugout Edge and Simple Fielding both name assistant coaches and parents as audiences, but those audiences need a way to receive plays. That points to share-by-link or export, not only showing the screen.
- Position assignment and rotation fairness is a real, separate pain point. It is a possible later feature, but it lies outside the whiteboard's core job.

### Gaps
- I found no Reddit, Facebook group, HSBaseballWeb, or Baseball Fever posts. These sources were not indexed by the search tool, so this gap reflects the method, not proof of absence.
- I found no survey data on how often coaches use whiteboards in each setting (dugout, practice, home).

## Recurring complaints in App Store/Google Play reviews

### Takeaway
Baseball-specific whiteboard apps have very few reviews, and many are abandoned. The main complaints are poor navigation, being unable to save or animate movement, no undo, file management that makes accidental deletion easy, purchases not syncing across devices, crashes after updates, and no Android version. I found no direct review evidence for complaints about small targets or clunky dragging.

### Cited Findings
- Baseball WhiteBoard (free, last updated 2017, 1.0★ from 1 rating). One review reads: "Terrible navigation, could be so much more. Can't save player movement, no tutorial, can't insert your own text" (5/7/2013). Another reads: "would be better if you could make a line without moving a player" (2009). — [App Store: Baseball WhiteBoard](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/baseball-whiteboard/id317282434)
- TacticalPad (multi-sport, 4.7★, 1.2K ratings, $24.99/yr subscription). One reviewer requested "an undo button" and better file management: "Make it more difficult to delete a folder vs a file" (7/4/2023). Another bought on iPhone, but the iPad "will not give me full access to the app!" (2020). Others reported a gray screen (2020) and that the app "doesn't open" after an update (2017). — [App Store: TacticalPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tacticalpad-coachs-whiteboard/id512949303)
- InMotion Softball Playbook (iOS) has 6 reviews averaging 3.0 and was last updated Aug 2021. Search results listed its price as $69.99. — [AppFollow listing](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/inmotion-softball-playbook/1018043002?country=us) / [AppAdvice](https://appadvice.com/app/inmotion-softball-playbook/1018043002) (from search summary; price not independently verified)
- MyPackagedDeal (softball, $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr) has a 3.0★ rating from 14 ratings. — [App Store: MyPackagedDeal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mypackageddeal/id1005659743) (from search summary)
- Baseball Force Out Teacher (iOS only). Commenters asked for Android: "Android please!", "please develop an Android version!", "who can afford iPhone? poor people like me use android". — [MLB Trade Rumors](https://www.mlbtraderumors.com/2020/03/new-app-for-youth-baseball-coaches-baseball-force-out-teacher.html)
- An independent review of Coach Tactic Board found the drill-builder unintuitive with "no export button." It called the app a supplement rather than a whiteboard replacement. — [Sports Technology Blog (2020)](https://sportstechnologyblog.com/2020/11/09/coach-tactic-board-review-can-this-app-replace-a-coachs-white-board/) (from search summary)
- In Pocket Coach, removing a pitch count requires holding the button "for about 2-3 second for it to remove ONE count" (4/23/2022). This is a touch-interaction friction complaint. — [App Store: Pocket Coach](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id633644009?l=en-us)
- GameChanger says premium features are free for coaches and staff and that fans pay, with prices that vary by listing ($14.99–$22.99/mo). One complaint summary is AI-generated (marlvel.ai) and unverified, so I give it little weight. — [App Store: GameChanger](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gamechanger/id1308415878)

### Inferences
- The baseball/softball whiteboard niche looks poorly served by maintained, well-reviewed apps. Abandonment (2017 and 2021 last updates) is itself a pain point that a maintained PWA can address.
- Undo and protection from accidental deletion of saved plays are named requests. Diamondboard already has undo, so a confirm step or "recently deleted" area for My Plays is a cheap follow-on.
- Cross-device purchase problems and iOS-only availability lead to complaints. A PWA with no purchase and no platform lock avoids both.
- The ability to save and replay movement (the 2013 complaint) is a minimum requirement, and Diamondboard's step animation meets it.

### Gaps
- I found no Google Play review pages for baseball whiteboard apps.
- I found no verbatim reviews that mention small touch targets, clunky dragging, missing softball fields, ads, or offline failure. These may be real complaints, but they are unverified.
- I did not find the "Diamond Chalk" app named in the brief.

## What coaches ask for in forums: situations and teaching approaches

### Takeaway
Coaches want full coverage of off-ball movement (backups, cutoffs/relays, coverage on hits to the outfield). For young players they also want simple mental rules ("ball, base, or backup") that apply to every play. Competitors' preset libraries cluster on the same situations: runner states, bunts, squeezes, pickoffs, rundowns, cutoffs, and relays.

### Cited Findings
- DiscussFastpitch, "Coach defensive responsibilities" (May 2024). Gags teaches that every play means fielding the ball, covering a base, or backing up. juasmith suggested "just get them in a habit of moving every play to field the ball, cover a base, or back up a play" and skipping advanced relays at first. CoachJD warned that drilling base assignments can make kids run to a base instead of playing a nearby ball, and suggested "pretend the ball is a magnet." CoachCeetar recommended MLB Gameday's field view, which shows "every single player (tiny graphic) moving in real time," plus quick post-game huddles on confusing plays. cvsoftball's approach is "you need to do this and this is why...", repeated every practice. — [DiscussFastpitch thread](https://discussfastpitch.com/threads/coach-defensive-responsibilities.46209/)
- USA Baseball's Youth Baseball Skills Matrix (as cited in a league guide) includes a team-situational component for ages 10–12 built around "ball, base or backup." — [BBL Baseball Development Guidelines (PDF)](https://cdn1.sportngin.com/attachments/document/8348-3379948/BBL_Baseball_Development_Guidelines.pdf) (from search summary)
- Dugout Edge's 50+ preset library covers nobody on, runner on 1st, 2nd, or 3rd, multiple runners, and special plays (bunts, squeezes, pickoffs, rundowns, cutoffs, relays). It offers both full-play and frame-by-frame viewing. — [Dugout Edge](https://www.dugoutedge.com/chalkboard)
- Simple Fielding includes softball-specific content such as slappers, the look-back rule, and 8U softball. — [Simple Fielding](https://simplefielding.com/)
- The Force Out Teacher creator says kids make a good play on a grounder and then throw to or step on the wrong base. He calls knowing the force outs "crucial." — [MLB Trade Rumors](https://www.mlbtraderumors.com/2020/03/new-app-for-youth-baseball-coaches-baseball-force-out-teacher.html)
- Baseball Positive (a coaching site) gives a default rule for a confused player: run the ball toward the middle of the infield (the pitching rubber). It also says to remind kids there's no rule against carrying the ball. — [Baseball Positive](https://baseballpositive.squarespace.com/core-concepts) (from search summary)

### Inferences
- Diamondboard's library should make "ball / base / backup" visible on every play, for example by color-coding each fielder's role per step and adding a notes template. Softball variants (look-back rule, slappers, 8U) are differentiators.
- A timed full-play mode plus a frame-by-frame mode (step animation) matches what competitors and coaches value.
- A per-step note template of "what + why" matches cvsoftball's "you need to do this and this is why."

### Gaps
- I found no forum evidence on demand for signals/signs features or first-and-third defense specifically, beyond their presence in vendor libraries.

## How players best learn defensive responsibilities

### Takeaway
The pattern that competitors and coaches converge on is to pause the play at a decision point, ask "where do you go?", then show the answer animated with the reason. It is backed by quiz-style apps, by coaches favoring repetition with the reason explained, and by suggestions to vary one variable at a time (outs). I found no formal research from ABCA, NFHS, or PCA on app-based teaching.

### Cited Findings
- Simple Fielding's "fielding lessons" stop each play so the player decides where to go, then grade whether they headed the right way. Coaches can send any play as a link. — [Simple Fielding](https://simplefielding.com/)
- Fielder's Choice claims 1,900+ scenarios across five difficulty levels. Baseball Academics has a player pick one of nine positions and decide where to throw. Where's The Play? targets ages 5–12 for practice when field time is limited or weather is bad. — [RAWG: Fielder's Choice](https://rawg.io/games/fielders-choice); [Engadget (2015)](https://www.engadget.com/2015-01-30-learn-the-mental-part-of-defense-with-baseball-academics.html); [Where's The Play?](https://mwm.ai/apps/where-s-the-play/6642695702) (vendor descriptions)
- A DiscussFastpitch poster said BASIQs is good for situational awareness but quiz-based rather than diagram-based. That implies coaches see quizzes and diagrams as complementary. — [DiscussFastpitch](https://discussfastpitch.com/threads/good-resource-for-learning-how-to-coach-moving-without-the-ball.46674/)
- Non-official coaching blogs recommend repeating the same play while changing only the number of outs, asking players about specific plays, and starting situational teaching early. — [breakingac.com (2025)](https://breakingac.com/news/2025/jun/10/developing-baseball-iq-in-young-players) (low-authority blog; from search summary)
- Mind & Muscle (a self-promoting vendor, July 2026) argues that "Knowing the right play and making the right play under pressure are two different things." It also argues that stat apps (GameChanger, iScore) record what happened but don't teach what should happen. — [Mind & Muscle](https://mindandmuscle.ai/situational-baseball-app)
- The DiscussFastpitch coaches favored the 3 B's framework, the "magnet" analogy, post-game huddles, and watching real games that show off-ball movement. — [DiscussFastpitch](https://discussfastpitch.com/threads/coach-defensive-responsibilities.46209/)

### Inferences
- A "pause and predict" player mode is the most commonly cited learning pattern and a gap in pure whiteboards. In it, a step pauses and the player taps where their position should go, then the animation and coach note reveal the answer. Diamondboard's step model and notes could support it without a backend.
- Assigned homework requires a way to get plays to players. Without accounts, a share link that encodes the play (URL hash) is the matching approach, and Simple Fielding already offers it.

### Gaps
- I found no ABCA, NFHS, or Positive Coaching Alliance guidance on teaching situational awareness through apps or diagrams.
- I found no evidence on gamification (badges, streaks) for youth defense learning beyond vendor claims.

## Willingness to pay and preferred pricing

### Takeaway
Prices span free (Simple Fielding, Dugout Edge's public board, Baseball Positive), one-time $4.99–$69.99, and subscriptions of $24.99–$99.99 per year. The strongest free competitor explicitly markets "no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions." Direct evidence of coach willingness to pay is sparse. One reviewer of a $4.99 app at first thought it "a little expensive."

### Cited Findings
- Pocket Coach costs $4.99 one-time. A reviewer wrote, "I originally though paying for this app was a little expensive," but later found it worth it (4/15/2022). — [App Store: Pocket Coach](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id633644009?l=en-us)
- Baseball Board costs $4.99 (iPad/iPhone) and exports diagrams as images or PDFs. — [App Store: Baseball Board](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6751254549) (from search summary)
- TacticalPad charges $24.99/yr, with video export of animated plays. — [App Store: TacticalPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tacticalpad-coachs-whiteboard/id512949303)
- PlayBook – Coach Tactic Board (multi-sport) lists $9.99, $49.99, and $179.99 tiers, including a lifetime option. — [AppAdvice](https://appadvice.com/app/playbook-coach-tactic-board/1603572641) (from search summary)
- MyPackagedDeal (softball) charges $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mypackageddeal/id1005659743) (from search summary)
- Mind & Muscle charges $9.99/mo or $99.99/yr. The same vendor page lists Coach's Eye at $39.99/yr and Hudl from $40/mo for teams. — [Mind & Muscle](https://mindandmuscle.ai/situational-baseball-app) (vendor-authored)
- InMotion Playbooks sells subscriptions, and free users "can review each scenario for a few days before the link expires." — [DiscussFastpitch](https://discussfastpitch.com/threads/good-resource-for-learning-how-to-coach-moving-without-the-ball.46674/); [InMotion](https://inmotionplaybooks.com/)
- Simple Fielding is free with "no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions" and an optional "Buy me a coffee." — [Simple Fielding](https://simplefielding.com/)
- Dugout Edge's chalkboard is free, while saved plays, playbooks, rosters, and schedules are premium (beta). — [Dugout Edge](https://www.dugoutedge.com/chalkboard)
- GameChanger gives coaches premium features free and charges fans. — [App Store: GameChanger](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gamechanger/id1308415878)

### Inferences
- Rec and youth volunteer coaches face free competitors and a free-for-coaches norm (GameChanger). Pricing a client-side PWA as a subscription would be hard to justify. Free, or a small one-time unlock (about $5–$10), fits the evidence best. Higher willingness to pay probably exists among high school and travel programs that buy InMotion or Hudl, but I found no direct evidence of it.
- Simple Fielding (free, browser-based, iPad-first, softball, share links, quizzes) is the closest direct competitor to Diamondboard's positioning. The feature priorities it implies are share links, a player quiz mode, and softball depth.

### Gaps
- I found no survey or forum thread that directly addresses one-time versus subscription preference among baseball/softball coaches.
- I found no team or league pricing data for whiteboard apps specifically.
